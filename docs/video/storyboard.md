## Production Notes

### Narration

- **Voice**: Clear, confident, moderate pace (not rushed)
- **ELI5**: Slightly warmer, conversational tone. Imagine explaining to a smart friend who is not a developer.
- **Deep Dive**: More authoritative but still approachable. Imagine presenting at a developer conference.
- **Pauses**: Brief natural pauses at scene transitions (0.3-0.5s)
- **Emphasis words**: "autonomous", "done", "self-maintains", "your choice", "70 percent"

### Music

- **ELI5**: Light lo-fi electronic, 90-100 BPM, positive energy throughout
- **Deep Dive**: Ambient electronic that builds:
  - Section A (Problem): Minimal, slightly tense undertone
  - Section B (Intro): Melody enters, energy rises
  - Section C (How): Steady, confident rhythm
  - Section D (Differentiator): Peak energy, most prominent music
  - Section E (Vision/CTA): Warm resolution, fades under final narration

### Video Assembly Order

1. Generate all 28 images via Nano Banana
2. Record narration audio for each scene (TTS or human)
3. Assemble in video editor:
   a. Place images on timeline matching durations
   b. Apply transitions as specified
   c. Overlay text titles where needed
   d. Add background music track
   e. Sync narration audio
4. Export at 1080p, 30fps
5. Review and adjust timing
