## Stale-content patterns

Three recurring patterns explain most of the staleness:

1. **Pre-session story counts** — Several wiki pages (Epic 1.5, Epic 12, Epic 18) reflect the original story set from the December 2025 / March 2026 planning, before Wave-2 and the Epic 28/29/30/31/33 sprint added new stories. Wave-2 added: 12-5/12-6/12-7, 18-6/18-7/18-8, 9-12, the entire 1.5-16..1.5-45 secret-management/observability/LLM-safe-ops track, and Story 19-6.
2. **Status drift on partially-implemented epics** — Epic 19 most prominently: wiki still says "1 done, 1 in progress, 3 drafted" while the epic actually completed during the auth-foundation sprint. Epic 17 shows "Drafted" without acknowledging Phase-3 RLS scaffolding shipped. Epic 22 doesn't note that its main deliverable (`IAgentExecutor`) was absorbed by Epic 19.
3. **Missing recently-scoped epics** — Epics 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 33 exist as briefs in `docs/stories/` and have root-level wiki topic pages (Secret-Management.md, Multi-Tenant-Provisioning.md, Multi-Git-Platform.md, Identity-Providers.md) but no `wiki/Epics/Epic-NN-*.md` detail pages. Home.md lists them under "Newly Scoped (this sprint)" but `wiki/Epics.md` index still says "All 27 epics" without the new entries.
